Make the Unheard Matter.

Silent Noise E.S.T. is an artist management and strategy agency for musicians, performers, image-makers, and cultural practitioners working outside the expected signal.

We work with artists who treat sound as material, performance as language, and the future as an unfinished composition.
Our signal We build long-term, artist-led careers for people who are not interested in fitting neatly into a genre, platform, or standard release cycle.
Our approach Management is not administration alone. It is positioning, protection, creative pressure, patient planning, and choosing the right rooms.
Our network From independent labels and listening rooms to museums, festivals, publishers, fashion houses, and underground radio, we connect ideas to context.

Artists in active transmission.

A small roster, intentionally held. Each practice is distinct; each relationship is built for the long frequency.

Sound artist / Composer

Vera Static

Vera Static builds dense tactile compositions from degraded radio, prepared strings, field recordings, and obsolete domestic electronics. Her live sets sit somewhere between ritual, transmission, and weather.

Management: Marcus Ellery →
Performance / Moving image

Oren Vale

Oren Vale creates slow-burn performances using choreographed light, amplified breathing, analogue projection, and architectural space. Their work has appeared in temporary cinemas, galleries, and former civic halls.

Management: Amelia Rook →
Producer / Interdisciplinary artist

Chosen Lio

Chosen Lio is a very mystical artistic project pushing bondaries at their limit led by Lio Dubois. We agreed to let her artistic freedom and we don't "A&R" her projects.

Management: Helena Voss →
Noise duo / Live electronics

Soft Collapse

A two-person collision of unstable rhythm, modified instruments, homemade circuitry, and industrial percussion. No two performances have the same internal logic or physical configuration.

Management: Marcus Ellery →
